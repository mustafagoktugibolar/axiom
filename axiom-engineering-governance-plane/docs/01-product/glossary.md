# Glossary

**Agent / Harness** — AI coding environment that plans or changes software.

**Governance Plane** — Organization-wide layer exposing accepted engineering intent and enforcement to humans/tools.

**Governance Record** — Versioned unit of engineering authority or guidance.

**Decision / ADR** — Accepted architectural choice with context, rationale, consequences, scope, and lifecycle.

**Invariant** — Condition that must remain true.

**Standard** — Repeatable organization/team engineering requirement.

**Principle** — Broad engineering belief used to guide judgment.

**Goal** — Desired system quality or strategic direction.

**Exception** — Explicit, bounded, usually expiring permission to deviate.

**System Graph** — Typed graph of software entities and dependencies.

**Preflight** — Governance evaluation before design/editing.

**Design Gate** — Validation that a proposed technical approach is consistent with applicable governance.

**Diff Gate** — Validation of actual code/config changes.

**Receipt** — Immutable evidence of what governance snapshot and checks produced a verdict.

**Authoritative** — Human-approved and active for governance.

**Candidate** — Proposed/inferred but not authoritative.

**Deterministic Rule** — Mechanical check with reproducible output.

**Semantic Finding** — Probabilistic model-assisted analysis with explicit evidence.

**Significant Change** — Change whose risk/impact requires formal design/review.

**Snapshot** — Content-addressed view of governance state used for an evaluation.
